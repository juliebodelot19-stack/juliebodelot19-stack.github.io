SH. 06 · FINAL-YEAR PROJECT · 5TH YEAR · 2026 – 2027

Digitalising chemical product management in an engine shop

For Air France's Engine Division: replacing an Excel/VBA tool with a simple Power Apps application available to every technician, at the Orly and Paris-CDG sites.

Project in progress · scoping phase

CLIENT
Air France · Engine Division, Methods department
CONTEXT
Final-year project · IPSA Master's
TEAM
4 students · one semester
TOOLS
Power Apps · SharePoint

01 · THE CONTEXT

Sensitive products, fragile tracking

In engine maintenance shops, safety cabinets and refrigerators store chemical products (oils, solvents, adhesives, inspection products) subject to strict rules on storage, temperature and shelf life.

Today, tracking relies on an Excel workbook with macros and on paper sheets posted on the cabinets. The result: slow updates, displays that wear out, change requests by email with no traceability, and a tool limited to a single site.

02 · THE OBJECTIVES

Simplify the whole process, not just the tool

A Power Apps application

Developed locally, then deployed to the Air France environment.

Accessible to everyone

Open read access for every technician, including on the shop floor, with restricted editing rights.

Always up-to-date displays

QR codes on the cabinets to replace the paper sheets.

Traceable requests

A request and approval workflow for changes, instead of email exchanges.

Scalable but controlled

Add a cabinet or a product easily, without opening up uncontrolled fields.

One shared tool

The same tool for both sites, Orly and Paris-CDG.

03 · THE APPROACH

A milestone-driven project

Regular on-site meetings with the client, at Orly or Paris-CDG.

  2. ● NEXT STEP

  3. UPCOMING

  4. UPCOMING

  5. UPCOMING
