SH. 07 · SCHOOL PROJECT · PROJECT MANAGEMENT · 2025

Reorganising the tooling inventory of an engine maintenance site

A case study inspired by Air France Industries' engine maintenance: freeing up the workshops, making tool management reliable and supporting the growth of the activity.

CONTEXT
Project management course · IPSA
CASE STUDY
Engine maintenance · Air France Industries
TEAM
Group project of 6
METHODS
5M · 5S · Lean · Gantt

01 · THE PROBLEM

Workshops saturated with tooling

At the engine maintenance site, a huge tooling inventory clutters the production areas, well beyond their capacity. Many tools are lost, out of service or obsolete, and the tracking data is often wrong.

The consequences: time wasted looking for tools, engine jobs stopped for lack of tooling or space, and rigid work areas. All this while activity is set to grow sharply, with new engine types to take on.

Our mission: propose and plan a project to reorganise the tooling and restore the site's productivity.

02 · THE DIAGNOSIS

Root causes, with the 5M method

Environment

Not enough space: tooling takes over the production areas, which are no longer flexible.

Means

No reliable information system to know where a tool is and what condition it is in.

Methods

No shared standards or procedures: a heavy and unreliable tool supply process.

Manpower

A lack of discipline in how tools are used and put away.

Material

Defective, out-of-service or obsolete tools mixed in with working ones.

03 · THE SOLUTION

Keep the essentials at hand, store the rest

The principle: the most-used tools stay in the workshop, all the others move to a dedicated store in a free building and are delivered on demand.

A dedicated store

Create a store in another building to free up the production areas, using the height of the building to optimise space.

Inventory & 5S

Carry out a complete tooling inventory and sort it by condition: operational, out of service, to be recycled. Then reorganise the areas with the 5S method.

A tracking system

Build an information system to know the location and condition of every tool, and anticipate the needs of each job.

Delivery on demand

Each morning, every technician orders the tools for their day through an app. The store prepares the order, a contractor delivers it to the workstation, and the tools go back in the evening.

04 · THE APPROACH

A project split into five work packages

  1. WP 1

    Initiation

    Define the problems and objectives, validate the scope, create the initial deliverables.

  2. WP 2

    Planning

    Structure the team, define the work packages and milestones, set up the tools.

  3. WP 3

    Design

    Tooling inventory, mapping of the current process, design of the information system.

  4. WP 4

    Implementation

    5S reorganisation, test deployment of the system, fitting out the store.

  5. WP 5

    Closure

    Analysis of the KPIs, validation of the solutions, final report and communication.
